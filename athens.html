Greece, Athens

Rank: 1st place to visit

Athens, known as the birthplace of democracy.

This place has always been my number one (1) travel destination ever since I was in high school reading books about Ancient Greece. I was fascinated by the historical events, discoveries, inventions, and significant figures (Socrates, Plato, Aristotle, etc.) that this place was able to produce. I want to be able to see the architecture that showcases the exceptional skills of Greeks; I want to see the sculpture and the paintings that tells a story; I want to eat food I have never tasted before, I want to experience new cultural practices; I want to buy unique souvenir that will serve as memento of my experiences in Greece, Athens.
