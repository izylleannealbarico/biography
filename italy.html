Italy, Rome

Rank: 3nd place to visit

Rome, known for the Colosseum.

This place is my third (3) travel destination. The reason why I want to visit here is because of the important and significant historical landmark where host gladiatorial combat takes place. I want to be able to see the architecture that showcases the exceptional skills of Romans; I want to see the sculpture and the paintings that tells a story; I want to eat food I have never tasted before, I want to experience new cultural practices; I want to buy unique souvenir that will serve as memento of my experiences in Italy, Rome.
