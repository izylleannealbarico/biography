Bucket List

Top 10 things I want to achieve and experience in this lifetime (before I die)

1. I want to establish my own tech company and become CEO.

2. I want to contribute and create something that would last beyond my lifetime and would benefit generations of Filipinos.

3. I want to travel the world for work and pleasure.

4. I want to be able to work and collaborate with the best people in terms of expertise, leadership, and significant groundbreaking contributions.

5. I want to advance the tech industry of the Philippines by providing and creating the latest technology that no one else can offer, applicable in different industries both locally and globally.

6. I want to reach self-contentment by embodying my ideal self in both values and traits I desire and admire from people.

7. Having friends and people around me with whom I feel supported, valued, loved, trusted, respected, and inspired; and being met with sincerity and honesty.

8. I want to be a person with clear, uncompromising boundaries; someone who is not afraid to be selective, to cut people off, and, most importantly, not afraid of people leaving. I want to find peace and security for myself by choosing what best aligns with my standards, and I will not let others step on my boundaries.

9. I want to find someone I can share my life with, both in presence and in time. Someone who is my base so when I soar high in the sky, I have a ground to land on. Someone with whom I can share my day, my experiences, and my time, and create memories that enrich each other’s lives rather than take from them. Someone who has a life of their own beyond the relationship. We would tell each other stories of our journeys as we build the lives we envision for ourselves.

10. I want to be able to say that I have done what I could do in my deadbed. Now it's my time to end the story without regret but filled with satisfaction that I have lived a meaningful and fulfilling life.

Passion

By Izylle

I feel my heart racing,
My hands sweating,
My neck tingling,
My eyes glistening,
I feel exhilarated,
I feel determined,
I feel confident,
I feel the adrenaline, its all-consuming,
I feel passionate that it scares me to death.

For I have goals and plans bigger than myself,
and I am and am willing to be crazy to make it happen.

For I have the passion to fuel me, that makes pain and suffering into calling.
