Germany, Munich

Rank: 2nd place to visit

Munich, known for the Gothic-style New Town Hall (Neues Rathaus) located in Marienplatz square.

This place is my second (2) travel destination. The reason why I want to visit here is because this is where Albert Einstein studied in elementary school. I want to explore and experience the place he grew up to. The architecture of the infrastructures, the arts from the museum, to eat delicious German food, experience new culture, and collect mementos as a souvenir to remember my trip to Germany, Munich.
